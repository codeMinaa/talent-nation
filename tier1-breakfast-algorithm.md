# TIER 1 — THE LITERAL ASSISTANT
## Mission: Make Me Breakfast

### 1. Ingredients Required
- One loaf of bread.
- Two eggs.
- Salt.
- Three tablespoons of butter.

### 2. Location of Ingredients
- Check the refrigerator for the bread and eggs.
- Get the butter from the refrigerator.
- Get the salt from the kitchen cabinet.

### 3. Equipment Required
- A frying pan.
- A plate.
- An egg whisk.
- A kitchen knife.
- A heater or stove.

### 4. Preparation Instructions
1. Remove the bread and eggs from the refrigerator.
2. Get the butter, salt, and required kitchen equipment.
3. Crack the two eggs into a bowl.
4. Add a pinch of salt and whisk the eggs.
5. Use the knife to slice the bread open in the middle.

### 5. Cooking Instructions
1. Place the frying pan on the heater.
2. Turn on the heater and set it to low heat.
3. Add three tablespoons of butter to the pan.
4. Allow the butter to melt.
5. Pour the seasoned eggs into the pan.
6. Fry the eggs until they are fully cooked.

### 6. Serving Instructions
1. Place the cooked eggs inside the sliced bread.
2. Place the prepared bread and egg on a plate.
3. Place the plate on the dining table.
4. Turn off the heater.

**End of Mission: Breakfast Prepared and Served.**

### Reasoning Challenge

The original instruction, "Make me breakfast," required several assumptions.

Some of the missing information included:
- The type of breakfast to prepare.
- The ingredients and their quantities.
- Where to find the ingredients.
- The equipment required.
- The order in which the tasks should be performed.
- How the food should be prepared.
- Where the finished breakfast should be served.

**Conclusion:**

A computer cannot reliably interpret vague instructions like a human. It needs clear, specific, and properly arranged instructions to perform a task correctly.

# TIER 5 — ONLINE STORE SYSTEM

## 1. Introduction

The purpose of this tier is to understand how an online store system works and how a computer receives, processes, stores and produces information.

## 2. Online Store Scenario

Imagine a customer wants to purchase three school bags from an online store.

The details are:

| Information | Value |
|---|---|
| Product | School bag |
| Price | ₦8,000 |
| Quantity | 3 |

## 3. Calculating the Total Cost

The online store calculates the total cost using multiplication.

**Formula:**

Total Cost = Price × Quantity

**Calculation:**

₦8,000 × 3 = ₦24,000

Therefore, the total cost of the three school bags is **₦24,000**.

## 4. Who Performs the Calculation?

The computer program performs the calculation by following instructions written by a programmer.

The customer provides the information, while the program processes it and calculates the total cost.

## 5. Information Stored by the Online Store

After a customer places an order, the online store should store important information such as:

1. Customer's name.
2. Delivery address.
3. Contact information.
4. Product purchased.
5. Quantity ordered.
6. Total amount paid.
7. Order or payment status.

This information helps the store manage orders and deliver products correctly.

## 6. Understanding Input, Processing and Output

An online store follows a sequence of steps:

| Stage | Explanation | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Input | Information provided by the customer | Product, quantity and delivery details |
| Processing | The program follows instructions and performs calculations | ₦8,000 × 3 |
| Stored Data | Important customer and order information is saved | Customer details and order records |
| Output | The result displayed by the computer | ₦24,000 and order confirmation |

## 7. Example of the Final Output

After successfully placing the order, the online store displays:

- Product: School bag
- Quantity: 3
- Total: ₦24,000
- Status: Order confirmed.

This information is the output because it is the result produced after processing the customer's input.

## 8. Key Lessons Learned

- Customers provide input to computer systems.
- Computer programs follow instructions to process information.
- Programs can perform calculations.
- Systems store important information for future use.
- Output is the result produced after processing.
- An online store uses input, processing, stored data and output to complete an order.

## 9. Conclusion

I have learned that an online store works by receiving information from customers, processing that information through a computer program, storing important order details and producing a result.

This shows how computers use instructions and data to perform real-world tasks.
